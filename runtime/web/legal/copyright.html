1. What this service does and does not host

The service stores what players create (mail, bulletin posts, saves). It does not host or distribute the original client software; players supply their own copies and they stay in the player’s browser.

2. Sending a notice of infringement

If you believe something on the service infringes your copyright, send a written notice to our designated agent (below) that includes, as 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) requires:

  1. a physical or electronic signature of a person authorised to act for the owner of the right;
  2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed (or a representative list, if one notice covers several works);
  3. identification of the material you claim is infringing, and enough information for us to find it (for example the account number or persona, and where it appears);
  4. your contact information: address, telephone number and email;
  5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
  6. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorised to act for the owner of the right.

4. What we do

When we receive a notice that substantially meets these requirements, we will remove or disable access to the material, and we will tell the user who posted it, with a copy of the notice (the notice may be shared with them, including your contact details).

5. Counter-notification

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you may send our agent a counter-notification under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g) that includes:

  1. your physical or electronic signature;
  2. identification of the material that was removed, and where it appeared before it was removed;
  3. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed because of a mistake or misidentification;
  4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if you are outside the United States, of any district where we can be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice.

We will pass the counter-notification to the person who complained. Unless they tell us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action against you, we will restore the material not less than 10 and not more than 14 business days after receiving the counter-notification.

6. Repeat infringers

Our policy is to end, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat infringers.

7. Misrepresentations

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly and materially misrepresents that material is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys’ fees. Please do not send a notice unless you mean it.
